Creator Radar may store registered interests, YouTube channel information, monitoring settings, language settings, and preferences in browser local storage.

Creator Radar does not require account creation or login. Settings are stored primarily in your browser and are not sent to or sold by the developer server.

Creator Radar may send requests to YouTube or public RSS addresses to check the latest public content from registered public YouTube channels.
