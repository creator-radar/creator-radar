Frequently Asked Questions

What is Creator Radar?

Creator Radar is a Chrome extension that helps you register interests and official YouTube channels and check for new content.

Where is my data stored?

Current settings and monitoring information are primarily stored in your browser local storage.

Does it find every news item?

No. It checks registered public channels and supported sources.
